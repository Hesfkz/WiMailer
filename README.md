## 🖥️ What is WiMailer?

WiMailer is a friendly, browser-based tool that helps you create professional-looking certificates and send them to many people at once. Whether you're organizing a workshop, running a course, or hosting an event, WiMailer makes certificate distribution simple - no design skills or technical knowledge needed!

**Imagine this:** You have a beautiful certificate design (a picture file) and a list of participants (a spreadsheet). ## 🎯 How to Use WiMailer - A Quick Walkthrough

### 1. Prepare Your Files
- **Certificate Design:** Any image file (PNG, JPG, WebP) you want as background. You can create one in PowerPoint, Canva, or use a template.
- **Participant List:** An Excel file or CSV with columns. At minimum, have a column for names. You can add more columns like email, course name, date, etc.

### 2. Upload Your Certificate
Click the upload area and select your certificate image. It will appear as the canvas.

### 3. Import Your Data
Click "Import" and select your Excel or CSV file. WiMailer will read all columns - each one becomes a draggable text box on your certificate.

### 4. Design Your Certificate
- Drag each text field (like "Recipient Name") to its position on the certificate
- Click any text field to customize font, size, color, and effects
- Use the data table at the bottom to click through different recipients and see how their certificates look

### 5. Make Individual Adjustments (Optional)
If someone has a very long name that doesn't fit, click their row in the table and adjust font size or position just for them.

### 6. Download or Send
- **Download:** Click "Export ZIP" to save all certificates to your computer
- **Email:** Click "Send Emails". Enter your SMTP details (for Gmail: smtp.gmail.com, port 587, your email address and app password). WiMailer will send each person their certificate automatically.

## 💡 Helpful Tips

- **Use high-resolution images:** 1920x1080 pixels or larger works best for crisp certificates.

- **Keep your browser open:** While sending bulk emails, don't close the browser tab until it finishes.
